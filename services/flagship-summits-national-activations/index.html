Do you design in-person or virtual events?

Both. Recent work includes a fully virtual 7-day summit with 100+ speakers and an in-person summit for 2,000+ attendees.

Do you manage logistics and production?

I lead strategy, content, partners and marketing, and coordinate production and logistics teams or agencies to deliver the experience.

How early should we start?

Ideally three to four months before the event, to secure speakers and partners and run a full campaign.

How do you measure success?

Against targets agreed at the start: registrations, attendance, engagement, partner value and post-event outcomes.
