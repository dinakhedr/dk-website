Brand Positioning & Architecture

A brand that says the right thing to the right audience — consistently, across every touchpoint.

Book a consultation See related work

Who it's for

This service is the right fit when one or more of these sounds familiar.

  • Your offer has evolved, but your brand still reflects where you started.
  • Different teams describe the company in different ways.
  • You are rebranding, renaming or merging products under one brand.
  • You serve both consumers and businesses and need a clear narrative for each.

What you get

  • Positioning strategyYour category, audience, differentiation and the proof behind it.
  • Messaging architectureCore narrative, value pillars and audience-specific messages.
  • Brand architectureHow products, sub-brands and offers relate to one another.
  • Naming & tone of voiceName development where needed, and a voice the team can apply.
  • Identity briefDirection for designers on logo, typography, color and imagery.
  • Launch planHow the new brand is introduced to customers, partners and the team.

How it works

Typically 4–10 weeks
01

Diagnose

Audit of the current brand, competitors and customer perception.

02

Architect

Positioning, messaging and brand architecture, tested with stakeholders.

03

Express

Naming, tone of voice and identity direction, working with your designers.

04

Launch

Roll-out plan, internal alignment and first-wave communications.
