Fractional Growth Leadership

Senior marketing and growth leadership, embedded in your team — without a full-time executive hire.

Book a consultation See related work

Who it's for

This service is the right fit when one or more of these sounds familiar.

  • You need senior marketing leadership but are not ready for a full-time hire.
  • Your team is capable but needs direction, priorities and standards.
  • You are between leaders and cannot afford to lose momentum.
  • You want to build a marketing function that scales with the business.

What you get

  • Growth strategy & planA clear plan aligned with commercial targets.
  • Team leadershipPriorities, rituals, standards and coaching for your marketers.
  • Campaign oversightDirection and quality control across channels and agencies.
  • Marketing operationsTools, workflows and AI-enabled processes that save time.
  • Reporting & KPIsRegular, transparent reporting to founders and the board.
  • Hiring supportRole design and selection when you are ready to hire full-time.

How much time do you commit each week?

Commitment is agreed upfront based on your stage and goals, typically two to three days a week, with clear availability for the team.

Do you work on-site or remotely?

Both. Most engagements combine remote work with regular on-site sessions in Cairo.

What happens when we are ready to hire full-time?

I help define the role, assess candidates and hand over the function, plans and playbooks so nothing is lost.

Can you lead agencies and freelancers as well as in-house staff?

Yes. I regularly direct agencies, freelancers and in-house teams together under one plan.
