Who it's for

This service is the right fit when one or more of these sounds familiar.

  • You are preparing to launch a new product, venture or business line.
  • Growth has plateaued and the team needs a sharper focus.
  • You are entering a new segment, city or country in the region.
  • Investors or the board expect a credible, measurable growth plan.

What you get

  • Market & competitor analysisA grounded view of demand, competition and the gaps worth pursuing.
  • Target segments & customer profilesThe customers to prioritize, and why they will choose you.
  • Positioning & value propositionA sharp, defensible articulation of what you offer and to whom.
  • Channel & acquisition planThe right mix of channels, partnerships and campaigns for your stage.
  • 90-day launch roadmapSequenced actions, owners, budget and milestones.
  • KPI frameworkThe metrics that matter, with targets and a reporting rhythm.

How long does a go-to-market engagement take?

Most engagements run three to eight weeks, depending on the number of markets, the depth of research required and the availability of existing data.

Do you also support execution?

Yes. After the strategy is agreed, I can lead or support execution through a Project Engagement or Fractional Leadership, so the plan is carried through rather than handed over.

What do you need from us to begin?

Access to any existing data and research, a short list of stakeholders to interview, and clarity on the decision the strategy needs to inform.

Which sectors do you work in?

Employment, EdTech, health-tech, SaaS, service marketplaces, financial services and sports education, among others. The method is consistent; the sector context is built in from day one.
