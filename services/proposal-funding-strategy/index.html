Who it's for

This service is the right fit when one or more of these sounds familiar.

  • You are responding to a ministry, donor or international funding call.
  • Your idea is strong, but the proposal needs structure and evidence.
  • You need to coordinate inputs from several departments or partners.
  • Past proposals were not selected and you want to understand why.

What you get

  • Funding landscape reviewThe calls, funders and criteria that fit your goals.
  • Program logicObjectives, activities, outputs and theory of change.
  • Proposal narrativeA clear, persuasive document written to the evaluation criteria.
  • Budget & logframeA defensible budget and results framework.
  • Partner coordinationAligned inputs, letters and commitments from partners.
  • Submission & follow-upFinal review, submission and preparation for evaluation.

Which funders have you worked with?

Including the Ministry of Higher Education, the European Union, Erasmus+, the Sawiris Foundation, HSBC Egypt and HTW Dresden.

Can you work to a tight deadline?

Yes, when the scope allows. Earlier engagement always produces a stronger proposal, so please get in touch as soon as a call is announced.

Do you also deliver the program if funded?

I can, through Program Architecture & Delivery, which ensures that what was promised is what gets delivered.

Do you write proposals in Arabic?

The website is in English, but proposals can be prepared in English or Arabic depending on the funder's requirements.
