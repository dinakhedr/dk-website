How I Work

Clear thinking. Disciplined delivery.

Every engagement follows the same discipline: understand the situation fully, design for the realities on the ground, deliver alongside your team, and measure what matters. The format adapts to your stage, budget and timeline — the standard does not.

Book a consultation See the engagement models

What you can expect

Four commitments that hold for every client, whatever the size of the engagement.

  • Outcomes before activity. Success criteria are agreed at the start, and every piece of work points back to them.
  • Designed for reality. Strategies and programs are built around your market, stakeholders and constraints — not a template.
  • Senior attention throughout. You work with me directly, from the first conversation to the final report. Nothing is handed to a junior team.
  • Transparent by default. Regular reporting, early warnings when something slips, and honest recommendations, even when they are inconvenient.

A disciplined approach

Four phases · one standard
01

Diagnose

A clear-eyed view of your market, organization and stakeholders: what works, what does not, and why.

You receiveA diagnostic summary, key findings and agreed success criteria.

02

Architect

The strategy, framework or program design, built around your goals and the realities on the ground.

You receiveA strategy or program blueprint, roadmap, budget and KPI framework.

03

Deliver

Hands-on execution, launch and team enablement, working inside your organization, not beside it.

You receiveLaunched initiatives, managed partners, playbooks and a trained team.

04

Measure

Defined KPIs, transparent reporting and continuous refinement, so results stay visible and improve.

You receiveDashboards and progress reports for leadership, boards or funders.

Some clients engage me for a single phase, others for the full cycle. A Strategy Sprint covers Diagnose and Architect — and many clients continue into delivery from there.

From first conversation to kickoff

A simple, predictable path from your first message to the start of work.

  1. Discovery callThirty minutes to understand your situation, goals and constraints. No preparation needed.
  2. ProposalA written proposal with scope, approach, timeline and fee, within five business days.
  3. Agreement & kickoffWe confirm scope and success criteria, then begin with a structured kickoff.
  4. Working rhythmWeekly check-ins and a monthly progress report, adjusted to how your leadership prefers to work.

How do I know which engagement model is right?

The discovery call usually makes it clear. If the direction is still open, a Strategy Sprint is the natural first step.

Can we start small?

Yes. Many clients begin with a Strategy Sprint or a workshop and extend the engagement once the direction is set.

Do you work with organizations outside Egypt?

Yes. I work with international donors and partners, and engagements across the region are welcome.

Who will we actually work with?

Me, directly. Where specialist skills are needed, I bring in trusted collaborators and remain accountable for the result.

How soon can you start?

Usually within two to three weeks of an agreed proposal, depending on current commitments.
