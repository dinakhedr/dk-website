Engagement models

Every service can be delivered through the model that best fits your stage, budget and timeline.

2–4 weeks

Strategy Sprint

A focused engagement to diagnose the situation and set a clear direction.

Best for: Leaders facing a decision: a new market, a repositioning, a program concept.

1–6 months

Project Engagement

A defined scope delivered end to end.

Best for: A launch, a rebrand, a program design, a summit or a funding proposal.

3 months +

Fractional Leadership

Senior leadership embedded in your team on a part-time basis.

Best for: Growing companies that need senior growth leadership before a full-time hire.

Flexible

Advisory & Workshops

Working sessions, workshops and ongoing counsel for leaders and their teams.

Best for: Leadership teams that want an experienced sounding board, or a team that needs to align fast.

Working together

From the first conversation to the final handover.

  1. Discovery conversation 30 minutes to understand your context, ambition and constraints. No obligation.
  2. Proposal A short written proposal with scope, approach, timeline, engagement model and fees.
  3. Kick-off Stakeholders, data and ways of working agreed in the first week.
  4. Regular check-ins Progress against agreed milestones, with decisions made quickly.
  5. Close & handover Results, lessons and a clear plan for what comes next.

Working principles

  • Clarity before activityEvery engagement starts with a shared definition of the problem and of success.
  • Designed for deliveryStrategies and programs are built for the realities on the ground, not for the slide deck.
  • Measured from day oneKPIs are agreed at the start and reported transparently throughout.
  • Capability that staysYour team is involved throughout, so the work continues after the engagement ends.

Questions

Anything else? Send a message.

Do you work on-site or remotely?

Both. Most engagements combine remote work with regular on-site sessions in Cairo.

Which engagement model should we choose?

It depends on how clear the problem is and how much hands-on support you need. A Strategy Sprint suits a defined decision; a Project Engagement suits a defined deliverable; Fractional Leadership suits ongoing ownership. We agree the right model in the discovery conversation.

How are fees structured?

Fees depend on scope and engagement model. Every proposal states the scope, timeline and fee clearly before any work begins.

Do you work with organizations outside Egypt?

Yes. My work spans local and international partners, including the European Union, Google, HSBC and European universities.

Is our information kept confidential?

Yes. Client information is treated as confidential, and I am happy to sign a non-disclosure agreement before we begin.
