Privacy

Privacy notice

What this website collects, why, and the choices you have.

Last updated·November 2026

The website www.dinakhedr.com is run by Dina Khedr, an independent business and strategy consultant based in Cairo, Egypt. For any question about your information, write to info@dinakhedr.com.

Information you send me

When you use the contact form, you share your name, email, organization, role, the type of organization you represent, the services you are interested in, your timeline, an optional budget range and your message.

I use this information only to reply to your enquiry and, if we work together, to prepare and run the engagement. I do not sell it, rent it or use it for unrelated marketing.

The form is processed by my website host, Netlify, which stores submissions and forwards them to my email. I keep enquiries for as long as needed to respond and for normal business records, and then delete them.

Booking a consultation

The “Book a consultation” button opens my Google Calendar booking page. Information you enter there is handled by Google under its own privacy policy.

Website analytics

I use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used: which pages are visited, how visitors arrive, and whether they get in touch. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information such as pages viewed, approximate location (country and city), device and browser type, and the site that referred you. It does not tell me who you are, and it does not store full IP addresses. I keep analytics data for 14 months.

You can opt out at any time by blocking cookies in your browser or by installing Google’s opt-out browser add-on.

Technical services

To deliver the site securely and quickly, it is hosted by Netlify and protected by Cloudflare, which process standard technical data such as IP addresses to deliver pages and block attacks. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. These providers may process data outside Egypt, under their own safeguards.

Your rights

You can ask me to see, correct or delete the personal information I hold about you, or to stop using it. Write to info@dinakhedr.com and I will reply within 30 days. These rights apply under Egypt’s Personal Data Protection Law (No. 151 of 2020) and, for visitors in the European Union or the United Kingdom, under the GDPR.

Links to other sites

This site links to other websites, such as LinkedIn, YouTube and partner organizations. Their own privacy policies apply once you leave this site.

Changes

If this notice changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
