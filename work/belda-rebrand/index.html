Context

HomeSurv was a service marketplace whose name and identity no longer matched its ambition. It needed a brand that could carry the business into its next stage, and a launch plan to match.

My role

As Marketing Director at JTS, I led the rebrand of HomeSurv into Belda and designed its multi-phase go-to-market.

Approach

  • NamingA new name, Belda, built for the marketplace's next stage.
  • IdentityA new logo and visual identity.
  • Tone of voiceA consistent way of speaking to customers and service providers.
  • Multi-phase go-to-marketA staged launch plan for a two-sided service marketplace.

Results

BeldaA complete new brand — name, logo, identity and tone of voice
GTMA multi-phase go-to-market plan for the marketplace
