Belda — Rebrand & Market Launch

A full rebrand of HomeSurv into Belda, with a multi-phase go-to-market for a service marketplace.

Context

HomeSurv's brand no longer matched its ambition. A service marketplace needed a name and identity that customers and providers would trust — and a plan to reach both.

My role

As Marketing Director at JTS, I led the rebrand and the go-to-market strategy.

Approach

  1. PositioningA clear role for the brand in the service-marketplace category.
  2. NamingHomeSurv became Belda.
  3. Identity & voiceLogo, visual identity and tone of voice.
  4. Phased go-to-marketA launch sequenced in phases, targeting homeowners and businesses.

Results

  • A complete new brand: name, logo, identity and tone of voice.
  • A multi-phase go-to-market strategy for a marketplace serving homeowners and businesses.
