The objective

HomeSurv’s brand no longer matched its ambition. A marketplace growing beyond home surveys, into everyday services for homes and businesses, needed a name, identity and experience that customers and providers would trust, and a plan to reach both.

My role

As Marketing Director at JTS, I led the rebrand and the go-to-market strategy: positioning, naming, identity and tone of voice, the brand guideline, the launch content, and the new website, from UX and content structure to brand integration.

The brand guideline

An identity built on seamless connections

The emblem is a stylized “B” of interlinked lines: the seamless connection between clients and service providers. It carries a chain-link idea for trust, a subtle smile for customer satisfaction, and a pin-and-checkmark cue for finding trusted professionals fast.
