WUZZUF Internship Program

Egypt's first structured internship program, connecting universities and employers at national scale.

Context

Students lacked a reliable path into their first work experience, and employers lacked a structured way to find and host interns.

My role

I created the program and built the university and employer partnerships behind it.

Approach

  1. A structured modelA defined internship framework for students and employers alike.
  2. University partnershipsUniversities as channels to reach students at scale.
  3. Employer partnershipsCompanies enabled to recruit through WUZZUF.
  4. National scaleOperations built to sustain thousands of placements each year.

Results

  • 4,000+ annual internship placements.
  • University and employer partnerships enabling 25,000+ companies on WUZZUF.
