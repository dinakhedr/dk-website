Project 01

The knowledge base

50+help articles, written from scratch
13categories across the platform
2audiences: members and platform admins

The objective

Make BrainsMingle self-serve. Members should be able to run sessions, communities, courses and payments on their own, and organizations’ admins should be able to manage their platform without depending on the support or tech teams.

The strategy

Two audiences, two libraries

A public Help Centre for members and creators, and a separate Suite Admin documentation for organizations running their own branded platform.

Help Centremembers and creatorsSuite Admin docsorganizations

Organized around the product

Categories mirror what people actually do on BrainsMingle: Getting Started, Communities, Live Sessions, Speed Networking, Consultation Services, Courses & LMS, Forms & Registration and Payments.

Task-first writing

Every article answers one job, such as “Host a webinar” or “Connect Stripe and receive payouts”, step by step and in plain language.

Earning features in full

Payments, paid sessions, paid memberships, earnings and coupons documented completely, so members can earn on the platform without help.

Built to grow

One article template and a searchable structure, so every new feature can be documented without redesigning the library.

What I delivered

The full Help Centre and the Suite Admin documentation: architecture, every article, page design and the coded pages, handed to the tech team ready to deploy.

Project 02

The guest view: a full public website

30+pages, from the homepage to every product feature
B2C + B2Bindividuals, and organizations through BrainsMingle Suite
14audience pages: 6 for individuals, 8 for organizations

The objective

Give BrainsMingle a public front door that sells the whole platform. Visitors needed to understand in seconds what BrainsMingle is, who it is for and why it is different, then find the page that speaks to them and take the right next step: sign up as an individual, or book a Suite demo for their organization.

The strategy

A site built like a product

Three layers: the product (10 feature pages and a capabilities overview), the audiences (6 pages for experts, creators, professionals, communities, organizers and programs) and BrainsMingle Suite for organizations (8 sector pages for accelerators, universities, academies, workplaces, studios, conferences, foundations and networks).

Product11 feature pagesAudiences6 pagesSuite9 pages

Lead with one promise

“Where professionals meet, learn, and earn.” The whole product in one line, before any feature list.

Segment at the door

Two ways in: “I’m here for myself” for professionals, experts, creators and learners, and “I’m here for my organization” for accelerators, universities, academies, communities and event teams, leading to BrainsMingle Suite.

Show, don’t list

A live product preview in the hero and a capability strip: consultations, communities, Speed Networking, courses, events, forms, payments and live sessions.

Proof from the platform

Featured experts and featured communities already active on BrainsMingle, so visitors see real people before they sign up.

Own the differentiator

Speed Networking presented as the feature found only on BrainsMingle: timed one-to-one introductions, matched by what people do and need.
