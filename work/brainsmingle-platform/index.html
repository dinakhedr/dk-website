Knowledge Base & Guest View

Two separate product-marketing projects I led as Head of Product Marketing at BrainsMingle, a video-first professional network: a self-serve knowledge base for members and admins, and a guest view for first-time visitors.

Growth & BrandProduct marketing
01 · The knowledge base 02 · The guest view

Project 01

The knowledge base

50+help articles, written from scratch
13categories across the platform
2audiences: members and platform admins

The challenge

BrainsMingle does a lot in one place: live sessions, Speed Networking, communities, courses, consultations and payments. Members and admins needed to find answers on their own, without waiting for support.

What I built

  1. Help Centre for membersEight categories: Getting Started, Communities, Live Sessions, Speed Networking, Consultation Services, Courses & LMS, Forms & Registration and Payments.
  2. Suite Admin documentationInternal guides for running the platform: users and activity, rooms and communities, payments and billing, content and learning, and platform settings.
  3. Written around tasksEvery article starts from what the user wants to do, such as “Host a webinar” or “Connect Stripe and receive payouts”, with search across all of them.

Project 02

The guest view

B2C + B2Bone page for individuals and organizations
1promise: “Where professionals meet, learn, and earn.”
2ways in, one for each audience

The challenge

First-time visitors needed to see quickly what BrainsMingle offers, and whether it is for them or for their organization, before signing up.

What I built

  1. One promise“Where professionals meet, learn, and earn.” A single line that explains the whole platform.
  2. Two ways in“I’m here for myself” for professionals, experts, creators and learners; “I’m here for my organization” for accelerators, universities, academies, communities and event teams.
  3. Proof on the pageFeatured experts, featured communities, and Speed Networking presented as the feature found only on BrainsMingle.
