Knowledge Base & Guest View

Two product-marketing builds for a video-first professional network: a self-serve knowledge base for members and admins, and a guest view that explains the platform to individuals and organizations before they sign up.

Context

BrainsMingle does a lot in one place: live sessions, Speed Networking, communities, courses, consultations and payments. Members and admins needed to learn it without waiting for support, and first-time visitors needed to see quickly what it offers and whether it is for them or for their organization.

My role

Head of Product Marketing at BrainsMingle. I planned, wrote and built the knowledge base from scratch, and designed the guest view, including its positioning and messaging for both audiences.

01 · The knowledge base

Members · Admins
  1. Help Centre for members35 articles in 8 categories: Getting Started, Communities, Live Sessions, Speed Networking, Consultation Services, Courses & LMS, Forms & Registration and Payments.
  2. Suite Admin documentation16 internal guides in 5 categories: users and activity, rooms and communities, payments and billing, content and learning, and platform settings.
  3. Written around tasksEvery article starts from what the user wants to do, such as “Host a webinar” or “Connect Stripe and receive payouts”, with search across all of them.

02 · The guest view

B2C · B2B
  1. One promise“Where professionals meet, learn, and earn.” A single line that explains the whole platform.
  2. Two ways in“I’m here for myself” for professionals, experts, creators and learners; “I’m here for my organization” for accelerators, universities, academies, communities and event teams.
  3. Proof on the pageFeatured experts, featured communities, and Speed Networking presented as the feature found only on BrainsMingle.

Results

  • A complete self-serve knowledge base covering every part of the platform, for members and for admins.
  • A guest view that explains BrainsMingle to individuals and organizations before they sign up.
  • One positioning line and messaging for both audiences, B2C and B2B.
