WUZZUF Ecosystem Programs

Nine programs and partnerships that turned Egypt’s leading job site into a career partner for students, developers, startups, employers and national initiatives: from internships and startup matchmaking to Google’s Mobile Application Launchpad, Egypt’s first software tournament, developer meetups and WUZZUF Unlock ’18.

The objective

Close the gap between what Egypt’s graduates and young professionals can do and what employers hire for, and make WUZZUF more than a job board: a career partner that sources, assesses, trains, badges and connects talent with startups, companies and national programs.

My role

As Programs Manager, then Strategic Partnerships & Programs Manager at BasharSoft, the company behind WUZZUF and Forasna, I designed and ran WUZZUF’s programs and partnerships: concept notes and proposals, partner agreements, budgets, operations, campaigns, on-the-day delivery and post-event reports, with Google, MCIT, Udacity, TIEC, RiseUp, A2OJ, AlMakinah, Geek Factory, Egyptian Geeks and Facebook Developer Circles.

01 · Since 2016

WUZZUF Internships

Egypt’s first structured internship program on a national job site: a full, free offering that helps companies discover and hire high-potential interns, and gives students and fresh graduates a trusted place to start their careers.

  • Free internship posting for every employer, with free unlocks of applicants.
  • WUZZUF Badges, pre-screening questions and filters to find the best interns fast.
  • Free employer branding on the WUZZUF Internships community page.
  • Clear rules: defined tasks and duration, named companies, internal hiring only.
  • Category campaigns for software, mobile, networking, sales and more.
5,000+internship opportunities offered in the first year and a half
730internships posted January to August 2017, already more than all of 2016
4,165internship vacancies January to August 2017, up from 3,144 in all of 2016
96,000+applications January to August 2017

02 · 2016 · 2017 · 2018

Startup Talent & Co-founder Matchmaking at RiseUp Summit

The first event of its kind in Egypt: connecting early- and growth-stage startups with high-potential talent and senior professionals, for key roles or as co-founders, inside the region’s leading entrepreneurship summit. I wrote the concept, agreed the partnership with RiseUp, selected startups and talent, and ran it on the day.

RiseUp16, 9 December 2016, Oriental Hall, AUC Tahrir: a talk by WUZZUF’s CEO, a founders’ panel on hiring key talent, equity and culture, startup and talent introductions, then facilitated networking. It returned for RiseUp17 with senior professionals, and again at RiseUp18.

90+startups applied, with no paid ads
360+talent applicants, from senior executives to young talent
120+startups and professionals confirmed to attend
3editions, 2016 to 2018

03 · WUZZUF Startups

Startup Support Program

A framework to position startups as credible, structured employers: built on four pillars, from first hires to measurable contribution to employment.

Talent & hiring accelerationEmployer profiles, job postings and candidate access, unlimited internships, startup hiring weeks and matchmaking, and university career-centre links.
Employer-brand visibilityA startup badge, a Startup Jobs hub, sector directory listings, social posts, newsletter features and hiring campaigns.
Sustainability & growthFounder spotlights and community, structured meetups, and vetted legal, HR, finance, marketing and technology providers.
Data & national impactTracking of jobs created, internships and conversion to employment, with quarterly dashboards and annual impact reports.

04 · 2016–2017 · Google · MCIT · Udacity

Mobile Application Launchpad

Google’s Mobile Application Launchpad, with Egypt’s Ministry of Communications and IT and Udacity, trained Egyptian developers in Android. WUZZUF was its career partner, and I led our side of the partnership.

SourcingSourcing 1,200 developers for the program and promoting it and its graduates.
Online career facilitationA MAL badge on graduates’ WUZZUF profiles, a dedicated Android jobs page, and a tracking dashboard.
Offline career supportFive career talks and workshops on CVs, interviews and the mobile job market, regular follow-up calls, and two career fairs.
1,000graduates on WUZZUF with the MAL badge
1,100Android and mobile jobs posted by 200+ companies
14,000+job applications by MAL graduates
26,000+profile views of graduates by employers

The program closed on 28 March 2017 at AUC’s Ewart Hall, with Egypt’s Minister of Communications and IT and Google’s MENA Managing Director, where WUZZUF presented the career results and five graduates shared their stories.

05 · November 2016 · March 2017

Mobile Developer Career Fairs

The first mobile career events in Egypt, by Google and powered by WUZZUF. The second fair, on 18 March 2017 in New Cairo, added buses for graduates from three meeting points, a CV clinic, assessment offers, hiring-needs signs on every booth, and WUZZUF Inbox: a code-based tool that let employers save the profiles of candidates they met straight to their WUZZUF accounts.

80+hiring companies across both fairs
750+job opportunities
1,000+job seekers
4.7/5employers’ rating for organization at the second fair

06 · 2017 · With A2OJ

A2OJ Code Battle by WUZZUF

Egypt’s first national software tournament, built with A2OJ, the competitive-programming platform used by 14,000+ Egyptian developers. Students and professionals solved algorithm and data-structure problems in the style of top tech-company interviews. WUZZUF was the platinum partner: I shaped the proposal, ran the marketing and landing page, registration through WUZZUF profiles, venues, certificates and badges, and the link to hiring partners.

  1. 01

    Qualification

    A 24-hour online round, open to anyone in Egypt who can code.

  2. 02

    Round one

    A second 24-hour online round; the top 500 advance.

  3. 03

    Round two

    A timed two-hour round, by region, to qualify for the onsite semi-finals.

  4. 04

    Semi-finals

    Onsite in three cities in October 2017: Cairo at The Greek Campus, Alexandria, and Assiut at Silicon Waha.

  5. 05

    Final

    The top contestants met in Cairo, with prizes including top women and youngest coders.

150+semi-finalists across Cairo, Alexandria and Assiut
50+finalists
5rounds, online and onsite

07 · 2017

Training bootcamps

WUZZUF as career partner for two of Cairo’s leading coding bootcamps: promoting each program and its graduates, sourcing and filtering applicants through technical assessments and WUZZUF Badges, and supporting graduates into jobs.

AlMakinah · FireUp full-stack bootcamp

A 12-week, full-time immersive bootcamp: 600 hours from programming concepts to a full-stack web developer with a portfolio. WUZZUF ran the technical assessment that shortlisted applicants for FireUp in March 2017 and for FireUp 2.0 later that year, gave the students career sessions such as a Tech CV Writing workshop, and connected graduates with employers.

Geek Factory · iOS bootcamp

A 10-week, part-time iOS bootcamp from February 2017 for candidates with strong computer-science backgrounds, chosen through coding challenges and interviews, with hiring preparation and career networking with tech companies and startups.

08 · 2017 · TIEC · ITIDA · MCIT

Next Technology Leaders

Next Technology Leaders is Egypt’s national technology-learning initiative, led by MCIT and delivered by ITIDA’s Technology Innovation and Entrepreneurship Center (TIEC), building young people’s skills in ICT and electronics through courses on Coursera, Udacity and edX with study-group mentors. I agreed WUZZUF’s role as its career partner:

  • Recommending courses and programs based on market demand and the skills gap among job seekers.
  • Special badges on the WUZZUF profiles of certified graduates.
  • Promoting the program to WUZZUF’s job seekers and employers, and alerting graduates to matching jobs.
  • Career advice, assessments and tracking of graduates’ hiring.

09 · 2017

WUZZUF Meetups

A meetups program connecting developers and young professionals with industry leaders, run with Egypt’s developer communities. The Developer Circles from Facebook Cairo Meetup, supported by Egyptian Geeks and powered by WUZZUF, in August 2017 brought engineers from Facebook and leading Egyptian tech companies to talk about information security, building products that last, React and Redux, software architecture, production engineering and hiring front-end developers, with 630+ registrations. In November 2017, WUZZUF and AlMakinah organized the next Developer Circles Cairo Meetup together, at the American University in Cairo. WUZZUF Meetups also ran its own sessions, such as a Front-End Development Meetup with iHub on becoming a better web engineer, React fundamentals and what front-end development really is.

The finale · May 2018

WUZZUF Unlock ’18

The first event of its kind in Egypt: a gathering of top employers, partners and recruitment experts where WUZZUF unveiled its new platform and its goal of one million Egyptians hired by 2020. The program walked guests through the new WUZZUF, talk by talk.

The WUZZUF storyFrom a coffee shop in Dokki in 2009 to Egypt’s leading job site, and the vision of one million people hired every year.
The new employer experienceInbox 2.0, Invite to Apply, WUZZUF Assistant and team collaboration for recruiters and HR managers.
WUZZUF ReDirectSourcing quality candidates straight to companies’ own career sites.
Better job postsRecommended titles, salary ranges, descriptions, skills and pre-screening questions.
The new profileA redesigned, mobile-first profile built on research with job seekers, for complete, up-to-date data.
Customer successA network that grew from 5,000 employers in 2015 to 20,000 in 2018, across 60 sectors.
