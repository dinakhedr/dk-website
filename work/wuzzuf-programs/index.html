The objective

Close the gap between what Egypt’s graduates and young professionals can do and what employers hire for, by making WUZZUF more than a job board: a career partner that sources, trains, badges and connects talent with startups, companies and national programs.

My role

As Strategic Partnerships & Programs Manager at BasharSoft, the company behind WUZZUF and Forasna, I built and ran WUZZUF’s programs and partnerships: with universities and employers, startups and RiseUp, Google, MCIT and Udacity, TIEC, and training providers, through to WUZZUF Unlock ’18.

Spotlight · 2016–2017

Mobile Application Launchpad

Google’s Mobile Application Launchpad, with Egypt’s Ministry of Communications and IT and Udacity, trained Egyptian developers in Android. WUZZUF was its career partner: sourcing applicants, giving graduates a MAL badge on their profiles, a dedicated Android jobs page, career talks and workshops, regular follow-up, and the first mobile career events in Egypt.

1,200developers sourced for the program
1,000graduates on WUZZUF with the MAL badge
1,100Android and mobile jobs posted by 200+ companies
14,000+job applications by MAL graduates

Mobile Developer Career Fairs

November 2016 · March 2017

Two career fairs by Google, powered by WUZZUF, brought 80+ hiring companies, 750+ job opportunities and 1,000+ job seekers together. The second fair, on 18 March 2017 in New Cairo, added buses for graduates, a CV clinic, and WUZZUF Inbox: a code-based tool that let employers save the profiles of candidates they met straight to their WUZZUF accounts. Employers rated its organization 4.7 out of 5.

The finale · 2018

WUZZUF Unlock ’18

A gathering of Egypt’s top employers and recruitment experts, where WUZZUF unveiled its new platform, with a personalized job feed for professionals and faster candidate search for employers, and set the goal of one million Egyptians hired by 2020.
