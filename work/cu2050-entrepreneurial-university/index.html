CU2050 — Entrepreneurial University

A multi-pillar framework positioning Cairo University as Egypt's first Entrepreneurial University — selected by the Ministry of Higher Education.

Context

The Ministry of Higher Education invited universities to put forward ambitious frameworks for entrepreneurship and innovation. Cairo University needed a proposal equal to its scale and standing.

My role

As project manager, I architected the framework and led the four-month proposal process Cairo University submitted to the Ministry of Higher Education.

Approach

  1. VisionA clear ambition: Cairo University as Egypt's first Entrepreneurial University.
  2. Framework architectureInterlocking pillars spanning education, research, startups and partnerships.
  3. Stakeholder alignmentFaculties, leadership and partners aligned behind one submission.
  4. Proposal developmentA four-month process from concept to final submission.

Results

  • The framework was shortlisted and selected by the Ministry of Higher Education.
