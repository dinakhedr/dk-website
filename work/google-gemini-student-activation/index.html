Context

Google set out to put Gemini Pro in the hands of Egyptian students at scale and in a short window — requiring reach on campus and online at the same time.

My role

As Program Director at WUZZUF & Forasna, I designed and led the activation campaign within the Google × WUZZUF partnership.

Approach

  1. University partnershipsCairo University, Mansoura University, AUC and the Arab Academy.
  2. On-ground activationCampus presence that converted interest into sign-ups.
  3. Influencer reachStudent-relevant voices that built credibility quickly.
  4. Multi-channel digitalCoordinated campaigns that carried momentum beyond campus.

Results

  • 30,000+ student sign-ups across four universities.
