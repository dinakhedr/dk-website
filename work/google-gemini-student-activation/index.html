Google Gemini Pro Student Activation

A nationwide campaign that brought Google Gemini Pro, free for 12 months, to Egyptian university students. Four streams ran together for two months: digital, campus booths, a student ambassador network and hands-on workshops.

The objective

Google offered Gemini Pro free for 12 months to students. The goal was to drive real adoption among Egyptian university students and recent graduates within two months, by reaching them on campus and online at the same time, and showing them what Gemini can do for their studies and careers.

My role

As Program Director at WUZZUF & Forasna, I designed and led the activation end to end within the Google × WUZZUF partnership: strategy, channels, campus operations, the ambassador network, workshops and redemption tracking.

The approach

Four streams, one campaign

  1. Stream A

    Digital campaign

    Email, SMS, WhatsApp, in-app, social media and a landing page, plus a creator reel by Hany Shaker across five platforms.

  2. Stream B

    Ambassador program

    350 student ambassadors, coached by a team of trainers, carried the offer into their own campus networks and peer groups.

  3. Stream C

    On-campus activation

    Branded Gemini booths at Cairo University, three AAST campuses, Mansoura University and AUC, plus Cairo University’s AI Nexus.

  4. Stream D

    Workshops

    41 trainer-led workshops, 27 online and 14 on the ground, showing students how to use Gemini for study and career tasks.

Results

  • 30,000+ student sign-ups across the campaign.
  • 19,797 confirmed Gemini Pro redemptions on campus across Cairo University, AAST, Mansoura University and AUC.
  • 28,000+ tracked clicks to Google’s student offer from WUZZUF, Forasna and iCareer channels.
  • 350 student ambassadors, driving 8,395 sign-ups on WUZZUF.
  • 41 workshops delivered across universities in Cairo, Alexandria, the Delta and Upper Egypt.
  • 59.6K landing page visitors and 98.5K page views, with an average visit of four minutes.
