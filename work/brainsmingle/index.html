BrainsMingle — Growth & AI Summit

Positioning, demand generation and community growth for a fast-scaling AI platform, culminating in the region's largest virtual AI summit.

Context

BrainsMingle had a strong product and an ambitious vision, but needed positioning that set it apart for learners and organizations — and an audience to match.

My role

Head of Product Marketing. I owned positioning, messaging, demand generation and community growth, and led the marketing of the BrainsMingle AI Summit.

Approach

  1. Positioning & messaging architectureDistinct B2C and B2B narratives built on one clear platform story.
  2. Foundations for scaleA knowledge base built from scratch, and guest-view pages that let visitors experience the platform before signing up.
  3. AI-automated marketing workflowsContent and campaign operations a lean team could run at speed.
  4. The summit as a growth engineA free, fully virtual 7-day summit (10–16 October 2026) across five tracks with 100+ speakers and audiences from 60+ countries, positioned as the largest AI summit in MENA.

Results

  • Platform users grew from 5K to 39K.
  • LinkedIn followers grew from 4K to 11K.
  • Facebook followers grew from 50 to 5K.
  • Instagram followers grew from 30 to 1,163.
  • 20K summit registrations in one month of campaign.
  • Summit audiences from 60+ countries.
