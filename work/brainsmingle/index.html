The brief

BrainsMingle needed a moment that would put a young AI platform on the regional map. The ambition was not another event, but a week that brought together the people building, using and studying AI across the Arab world, and introduced them to each other.

My role

Head of Product Marketing at BrainsMingle. I led the summit end to end, from the first concept to the closing night: idea, brand, programme, speakers, partners, campaign and speaker experience.

  1. Concept & positioning“Everyone in AI. One week. One room.” Positioned as the largest AI summit in MENA.
  2. Brand identityThe summit name, logo and visual system, with a colour code for each track.
  3. Themes & five tracksL&D, Creative, Startups, Business and Tech, each with its own curated speakers.
  4. Programme architectureOpening and closing days, five nights of parallel tracks, a nightly Day Spotlight and Speed Networking.
  5. Speaker sourcing135 speakers confirmed and delivered, from 15 countries.
  6. Sponsors & partners90 sponsors and partners, including government, regional and strategic partners.
  7. Manus partnershipManus as AI enablement partner, and the Vibe Editing Hackathon built with it.
  8. Speaker experienceOnboarding for all 135 speakers and an interactive Speaker Handbook.
  9. Social campaignOne campaign on LinkedIn, Facebook and Instagram, with the same posts on all three — design, copy, publishing and moderation. 340 posts in 30 days.

The concept

Everyone in AI. One week. One room.

Five tracks, each built around a single domain with its own speakers and conversations, running in parallel every night of the summit.

AI in Learning & Development

How AI is transforming learning, from classrooms and universities to corporate training and the future of work.

Topics delivered

  • The future of L&D and redesigning it for an AI-native workforce
  • A practical AI blueprint for universities and graduate readiness
  • AI-assisted, accessible learning and the human skills AI can’t replace
  • Managing AI teams, personal branding and research integrity

AI in Creative Industries

How generative AI is reshaping design, content, media and entertainment, and what it means for human creativity.

Topics delivered

  • AI video for Arab brands, and how WUZZUF built a full campaign with AI
  • Music, cinema, gaming and the new rules of storytelling
  • Hands-on workshops: vibe coding, AI video ads and AI filmmaking
  • Designing human-centred AI products and the changing creative role

AI in Startups & Entrepreneurship

For everyone building, funding and scaling AI companies, from first idea to lasting business.

Topics delivered

  • From idea to MVP in days, and from AI product to AI company
  • MENA SaaS growth roadmaps and AI go-to-market stacks
  • Where AI creates real value in health, hiring and operations
  • Hubs, platforms and investment opportunities for AI solutions

AI in Business & Enterprise

AI as a business story: how it transforms every function, for the leaders and professionals navigating it.

Topics delivered

  • Why AI projects stall, and how to get real ROI at enterprise scale
  • AI in marketing, SEO, product marketing and B2B pipeline
  • AI in strategy and go-to-market for financial services
  • Trust, AI-ready UX and governed agentic AI for decisions

AI in Tech & Infrastructure

The full story of AI from silicon to software, and the infrastructure powering it.

Topics delivered

  • Building AI agents, from deep search to production
  • DevOps, AI-powered security reviews and ethical hacking
  • Video analytics, spatial and physical AI
  • Software architecture after cheap code, automation and the infrastructure shift

Speakers treated as customers

Every one of the 135 speakers was onboarded personally and given an interactive Speaker Handbook, designed so they could arrive ready, promote their session and get the most out of the week.

  • An 11-section handbook — welcome, the week at a glance, Speed Networking, profile, session day and after-session steps.
  • A personal session finder — each speaker's own date, time, track and co-speakers in one place.
  • A browser-based session room — no Zoom, no installs.
  • An 11-point pre-session checklist to go live with confidence.
  • Ready-to-share promotion assets so every speaker brought their own audience.
