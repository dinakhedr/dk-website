About

Engineer by training. Strategist by practice.

I'm Dina Khedr, an independent business and strategy consultant based in Cairo. For eighteen years I have worked where strategy meets execution — first building systems, then growing platforms, then designing programs at national scale.

My story

01

Engineering roots

I began as an engineer, with a B.Sc. in Communications & Electronics from Alexandria University and ITI's nine-month Software Development program. At Andalusia for Business Solutions, a healthcare IT company, I progressed from .NET developer to IT project manager to technical support manager — leading cross-functional teams of 50+ and a migration of 125,000+ medicine records. That foundation still shapes my work: systems thinking, structured delivery and respect for detail.

02

Growth across Egypt's tech platforms

I went on to lead operations, growth, partnerships and marketing across e-commerce, employment, transit tech, e-learning, SaaS, health-tech and service marketplaces — at Bkam, WUZZUF, Buseet, almentor, Daftra, Chefaa, JTS and BrainsMingle. Along the way I created Egypt's first structured internship program, launched Bkam in Saudi Arabia and Kuwait, rebuilt brands and grew a platform from 5K to 39K users.

03

Programs at national scale

At NilePreneurs, and later with Cairo University and WUZZUF, I designed and delivered accelerators, workforce programs, youth initiatives, mentor networks and national activations — with partners and funders including HSBC Egypt, the European Union, the Sawiris Foundation, HTW Dresden and Google.

04

Independent practice

Today I bring that combined experience to founders, institutions and development partners as an independent consultant — strategy that scales, programs that deliver.
